Miso the Cat — Data safety

Updated November 3, 2026

Privacy policySupport

This summary describes Android 1.3.3 (8), currently available for internal testing. It is our explanation of the app’s data practices, not a Google approval or a legal certification. Older Android and current iOS test builds have differences described below.

Current Android app

Data collected by MisoNo user data is sent to AL4Bro or an app analytics service by this build. Gameplay and purchase-access records are processed locally. Store payment processing, optional support emails and this website are explained separately below.
Data shared by MisoNo user data is sold or shared for advertising or profiling. There are no advertising or social-network SDKs in the app.
Accounts and communicationNo Miso account, chat, public profile or user-upload feature.
Local storageScores, stage progress, preferences, verified ownership and Android complimentary-access status stay on the device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase progress.
PurchasesA parent gate precedes native Google Play checkout. Google handles payment and store-account information. Miso receives signed purchase records to verify and restore access on the device; it does not receive card details or upload receipts to our servers.
Analytics and diagnosticsNo gameplay analytics or ads. Unity engine diagnostics and legacy cloud crash reporting are disabled in this Android build. Miso does not initialize Unity Gaming Services telemetry.
PermissionsThe Android build includes internet/network-state and Google Play billing permissions. It does not request advertising ID, location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access.
Deletion and questionsDelete local saves through Android’s app-data settings. For support information you have sent us, email atanaslabroski@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Store records are managed by Google. No Miso account exists to delete.

Store services, support and this website

Google and Apple process store purchases under their own policies. A parent who emails support supplies an email address and message voluntarily; we use these to respond, troubleshoot and meet applicable obligations. GitHub Pages may process technical request information such as IP addresses to serve this website. These are separate from local gameplay. Read our privacy policy for providers, retention and choices.

SDK details and earlier test builds

The app uses Unity IAP 5.3.1. Unity’s SDK documentation lists temporary diagnostic and performance metrics. Source and configuration review shows Miso does not initialize the Unity service that sends those metrics; that finding is not a claim that every app using this SDK collects nothing.

Earlier test versions used Unity IAP 5.4.4, which introduced purchase telemetry. Earlier Android builds and iOS test builds through 1.3.3 (9) also predate our explicit engine-diagnostics disabling. They may have transmitted purchase, technical or diagnostic information to Unity. We have not established whether individual earlier installations transmitted that data. The current iOS test build should not be described using the Android build’s no-diagnostics configuration.

Google Play declaration

For the current Android configuration, our intended Data safety response is no off-device user-data collection or sharing by the app. This is based on the app code, packaged permissions, SDK initialization paths and release settings. Local-only processing and payment details handled directly by Google Play have different treatment from app data collection. Any future SDK or data-practice change requires this summary and the Play declaration to be reviewed again. This page does not mean that the Play form has been submitted or approved.

References: Google’s Data safety definitions and Unity IAP documentation for versions before 5.4.
