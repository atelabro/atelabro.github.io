Miso the Cat — Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026 · iOS 1.3.3 (10) and Android 1.3.3 (8)

This policy explains how Miso the Cat, developed by AL4Bro (Atanas Labroski), handles information. It is written for parents and guardians. Miso is a collection of children's games with free activities and an optional one-time purchase.

Playing Miso

Miso does not ask children to create an account or enter their name, email address, birthday, or location. It has no chat, public profiles, user uploads, advertising, or behavioral advertising. We do not operate a gameplay server or collect gameplay analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress, scores, preferences, and a record of verified purchase access on your device. On Android, a complimentary review-access code can also store a local unlock flag; entering it does not create an account or send the code to our servers. This lets you continue playing and use previously verified access offline. Miso does not upload these local saves to our servers. Your device or store may provide backups under its own settings and policies. Clearing app data or removing the app may erase local progress.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are for grown-ups and are behind a parental gate. Apple or Google processes the payment through your store account. Miso receives purchase and entitlement information, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, signed purchase records, and purchase status, to verify access and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Current releases use Unity In-App Purchasing 5.3.1 for native Apple App Store and Google Play purchases. Miso checks purchase records on the device and does not send them to an AL4Bro server. Unity’s documentation for this SDK also describes temporary diagnostics and performance metrics. Miso does not initialize the Unity Gaming Services telemetry service that sends these metrics; this is separate from the engine-diagnostics setting described below. We do not enable Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, a Unity account sign-in, or a direct-to-consumer webshop in Miso.

Apple and Google handle store account, payment, fraud prevention, and store diagnostics information under their own policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. See also Unity's privacy information for IAP versions before 5.4.

Analytics and diagnostics

iOS version 1.3.3 (10) and Android version 1.3.3 (8) contain no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Unity engine diagnostic data collection, legacy analytics and crash reporting are all disabled in these builds, so the app does not send diagnostic, usage or device data to Unity or to us.

Earlier test builds (iOS through 1.3.3 (9) and earlier Android test builds) had a separate engine-diagnostics setting enabled and may have sent diagnostic or technical information to Unity. We have not established whether those builds actually transmitted such data. Unity handles any such data under its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Earlier test builds

Earlier test releases used Unity IAP 5.4.4, which introduced Unity purchase telemetry. Those versions may send purchase events, identifiers, and device information to Unity under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Update to the latest test release available for your platform. Contact us if you have a privacy request concerning an earlier test version.

Support messages

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the email address and information they choose to send. We use it to answer the request and troubleshoot the app. Please do not send personal information about a child, passwords, or payment-card details. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete it.

Children's privacy and your choices

We do not intentionally solicit personal information from children. Parents can contact us about access, correction, or deletion of information they have provided. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us so we can address the request and delete it where appropriate. The parental gate controls access to grown-up actions; it is not a request for consent to collect children's personal information.

You can remove local app information through your device's app-data controls. This does not cancel or refund a store purchase. Store transaction records and refund requests are managed by Apple or Google.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add tracking scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the website, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

For support or privacy requests, a parent or guardian can email atanaslabroski@gmail.com. We will update this page if Miso's practices change and show the updated effective date.
