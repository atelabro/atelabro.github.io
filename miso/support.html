Help for parents and grown-ups

Need a hand? Email atanaslabroski@gmail.com. Please include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or personal information about your child.

Unlocking the collection

Miso includes free games and an optional one-time purchase to unlock the full collection. There are no ads or subscriptions. Purchase controls are behind a grown-up gate. Your store displays the current local price before you confirm.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account that made the purchase. Open the locked button at the top of the collection or choose a locked game, complete the grown-up gate, and select Restore Purchases. You need an internet connection to check with the store. Once your purchase is recognized, the top button shows an open lock.

If a purchase is still waiting for approval, wait for the store to confirm it. If access is still missing, contact us before trying to purchase again. An Apple purchase does not transfer to Google Play, or vice versa.

Progress and reinstalling

Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. Restoring a purchase restores access to paid games, not saved progress. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase progress. Miso does not provide its own account or cloud-save service.

Sound and screen orientation

Use the speaker button on the collection screen to turn sound on or off. If you see the rotate-device picture, turn the device upright to continue that game.

If a game stops responding

Close and reopen Miso, then try restarting your device. Check your store for an update. Contact us if the problem continues; avoid deleting the app if you want to keep local progress.

Refunds

Purchases and refund requests are handled by the store where you purchased: Apple or Google Play.
