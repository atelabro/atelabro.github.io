Support / Поддршка

A playful Macedonian-language app for matching clock times. Browse today or the current week, save favourite meanings, enable optional reminders, and share an image.

Contact / Контакт

For help, questions, or privacy requests, email atanaslabroski@gmail.com. Please include your device model, app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

За помош или прашања, пишете на адресата погоре. Наведете го моделот на уредот, верзијата на апликацијата и краток опис на проблемот.

Reminders

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in your device settings. Reminders for liked entries are scheduled one minute before their time. The app also offers a Monday reminder to choose the new week’s favourites. Delivery can be affected by notification settings, Focus modes, and operating-system restrictions.

Time and calendar

Цагери uses Europe/Skopje time, including daylight-saving changes. The interface shows the current week. The calendar is bundled with the app and works offline.

Saved wishes and wording

Your likes and wording preferences are saved on your device. No account is required. You can unlike entries, change wording, or disable reminders in the app.

Sharing

Sharing creates an image and opens your device’s share options. Choose the destination yourself; the app does not post automatically.

Advertisements and privacy

The app uses Google AdMob banner ads and requests non-personalized advertising. Where required, consent controls appear before ads. The “Приватност на реклами” option is available in settings when the consent provider requires it.
