Privacy policy

Цагери / Cageri · Updated 30 September 2026

This policy describes data handling in the Цагери mobile app, provided by Atanas Labroski. Contact: atanaslabroski@gmail.com.

App data stored on your device

The calendar is included in the app. Liked entries, wording preferences, and reminder settings are stored locally. These are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app does not require an account and has no developer-operated backend. Device backups may include local app data according to your operating-system settings.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications for liked times and weekly reminders. These are scheduled on the device, not delivered through a developer-operated push server. You can disable reminders in the app or notification permissions in your device settings.

Sharing

If you choose to share an entry, an image is generated and passed to the destination you select through the system share interface. That destination handles the shared image under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

Цагери integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. It requests non-personalized ads and gathers consent where required before initializing ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve data collection for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics.

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app-related identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interaction information, crash information, and performance diagnostics. Advertising identifiers are subject to platform permissions and availability. The app does not request your precise GPS location or require your name or email to use its calendar.

Where the consent provider requires it, open “Приватност на реклами” in the app’s settings to review privacy choices. The calendar remains usable when ads are unavailable.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure, and Google’s Android SDK data disclosure. Google and its advertising partners may process data outside your country under their applicable policies.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove likes and change preferences in the app. To remove locally stored app data, delete the app (rather than offloading it on iOS) or clear its storage on Android. Manage any backups separately in your device settings. Google and other providers manage data they receive under their own policies and privacy controls; deleting this app does not automatically delete their records.

Audience

Цагери is an entertainment app, not a service designed for young children. Follow the age rating shown in your app store.

This support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the site involves normal web requests handled by GitHub; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics or advertising scripts to these pages.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
