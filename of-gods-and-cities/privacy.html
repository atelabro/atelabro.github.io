Playing and saving

The game stores your local display name, settings and saved towns on your device. You can play without signing into a cloud account. If you sign in, named saved towns, their names and modification information are synchronized through Unity Cloud Save and linked to your player identifier.

A previously signed-in account may resume and sync automatically. Signing out stops account use on that device; it does not delete existing cloud data. The game initializes Unity services when checking whether an account can resume, so signed-out play should not be understood as a guarantee of no service connections.

Accounts and service providers

Unity Player Accounts handles browser-based registration and sign-in, including your email and Unity account identifier. Unity Authentication provides the game with authentication identifiers and session tokens. The game does not provide its own password-entry form.

Unity provides account and cloud-storage services. Its services may process technical connection information and service diagnostics. See Unity’s privacy policy. App stores and your operating system may process their own account, purchase or diagnostic information under their respective policies.

Support and this website

If you contact support, your email and the information you choose to send are used to respond and investigate your request. Avoid sending passwords, login codes or unrelated personal information.

This site uses no added analytics, advertising scripts or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Why we process data

We use account identifiers and cloud saves to provide the optional synchronization you request. Support correspondence is used to answer your requests; technical service information supports reliability and security. We do not integrate advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and your choices

Local saves remain on your device until removed; backups or other devices may keep additional copies. Removing a local save while signed out does not remove its cloud copy. Cloud deletion requires a successful connection.

Versions with the Delete account option in the home profile menu provide a permanent game-account and cloud-save deletion action. It keeps local saves and pauses synchronization if cleanup fails. A new explicit sign-in can create a new game account and synchronize retained local saves. See account and data deletion for the distinction between game data and your Unity Player Account. You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of data associated with this game.
