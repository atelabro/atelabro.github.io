Account and data deletion

This page concerns Of Gods & Cities.

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion without installing or opening the game. Email atanaslabroski@gmail.com. Requests are handled manually after account ownership is verified; sending an email does not immediately delete data.

Delete within the game

If your installed version has no Delete account option, use the email route above.

On the home screen, select your leader name, then Delete account. Read the confirmation and choose Delete permanently. Close the game on other devices first. The game removes cloud save files before deleting its authentication account. A failed or interrupted deletion pauses synchronization; reconnect and retry. Local saves and backups remain on your devices and can be removed separately in Load. Your Unity Player Account is separate.

Request deletion without the app

Privacy contact: atanaslabroski@gmail.com, subject “Of Gods & Cities — data deletion”. Include your game player ID if available and the email used for your Unity Player Account; never send your password, session token or login code. We will need to verify the account before deleting its data.

Signing out is not deletion. Deleting an individual save is not deletion of the entire game account. Local saves and backups on your devices are separate from cloud copies.

Your Unity Player Account

Your Unity Player Account can be used with more than one game. Manage that separate account through Unity’s account portal. Deleting it does not automatically delete data held by integrated games, including Of Gods & Cities.

What deletion covers

The request concerns your Of Gods & Cities game account and its cloud save files. Local saves and backups on your devices, your separate Unity Player Account and correspondence sent to support are separate. Please specify if your request also concerns support correspondence. We will explain any data that cannot be removed and why when handling your request.
